# 8-Week Study Plan

Target: ~1–1.5 hours on weekdays, 2–3 hours on weekends. Adjust to your pace — the order matters more than the dates.

## Core 1 (Weeks 1–4)

| Week | Focus | Tasks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hardware (Domain 3) | Cables/connectors, RAM, storage, RAID, motherboards, CPUs, PSUs, printers. Do [labs/01-pc-build.md](labs/01-pc-build.md). |
| 2 | Networking (Domain 2) | Ports & protocols, TCP/UDP, IPv4/IPv6, DHCP/DNS, Wi-Fi standards, network devices, tools. Memorize [ports](cheat-sheets/ports-protocols.md). |
| 3 | Mobile + Virtualization/Cloud (Domains 1 & 4) | Laptop components, mobile connectivity, MDM, hypervisors, cloud models (IaaS/PaaS/SaaS). Do [labs/02-virtual-machine.md](labs/02-virtual-machine.md). |
| 4 | Troubleshooting (Domain 5) + review | Learn the [6-step methodology](cheat-sheets/troubleshooting-methodology.md) cold. Take 2 full practice exams, review every wrong answer. **Book Core 1.** |

## Core 2 (Weeks 5–8)

| Week | Focus | Tasks |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Operating Systems (Domain 1) | Windows editions, install/upgrade, Control Panel/Settings, CLI tools, macOS & Linux basics. Do [labs/03-windows-cli.md](labs/03-windows-cli.md). |
| 6 | Security (Domain 2) | Physical/logical security, malware types, social engineering, Windows security settings, wireless security, data destruction. |
| 7 | Software Troubleshooting + Operational Procedures (Domains 3 & 4) | Malware removal steps, OS/app issues, documentation, change management, backups, safety, professionalism, scripting basics. |
| 8 | Review | 2–3 full practice exams, flashcards daily, fix weak areas from PROGRESS.md. **Book Core 2.** |

## Daily routine
- 10 min: flashcards (`flashcards/*.csv` in Anki)
- 40–60 min: video/book + write notes in the domain file
- 10 min: `python3 practice/quiz.py -n 10`
- 1 min: `git commit -am "Day N: <topic>"`

## Recommended resources
- Official CompTIA exam objectives PDF (free) — the source of truth
- Professor Messer's free A+ video course
- Mike Meyers' A+ course/book, or the CompTIA Official Study Guide
- Jason Dion or Mike Meyers practice exams (great for performance-based questions)
