# Wi-Fi Standards

| Standard | Name | Frequency | Max speed (theoretical) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 802.11a | — | 5 GHz | 54 Mbps |
| 802.11b | — | 2.4 GHz | 11 Mbps |
| 802.11g | — | 2.4 GHz | 54 Mbps |
| 802.11n | Wi-Fi 4 | 2.4 / 5 GHz | 600 Mbps (MIMO) |
| 802.11ac | Wi-Fi 5 | 5 GHz | ~6.9 Gbps (MU-MIMO) |
| 802.11ax | Wi-Fi 6 / 6E | 2.4 / 5 / (6E: 6 GHz) | ~9.6 Gbps (OFDMA) |
| 802.11be | Wi-Fi 7 | 2.4 / 5 / 6 GHz | ~46 Gbps |

- 2.4 GHz non-overlapping channels: **1, 6, 11**
- 2.4 GHz = range & wall penetration; 5/6 GHz = speed, less interference
- Channel width: 20 / 40 / 80 / 160 (/320 on Wi-Fi 7) MHz — wider = faster, more interference
- Regulations: allowed channels vary by country
